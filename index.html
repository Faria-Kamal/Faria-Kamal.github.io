CHAPTER 01/ ABOUT

A computer science graduate moving deliberately into AI & data science.

My background is in Computer Science and Engineering, followed by roles in project coordination, IT systems support and ERP implementation. That combination matters: I've spent years asking stakeholders the right questions, documenting decisions and explaining technical issues clearly to non-technical people.

I'm now applying that same discipline to data work. My projects are intentionally small enough to understand end to end, but grounded in realistic data and honest limitations. I'd rather show a model that misses 59% of cancellations and explain why, than present a headline number without context.
